When and where?

Pick a date and any cities you'd like to include. We'll look up their current time zones for you. Keep in mind, the list of cities available is based on what your browser supports.

We also take care of things like Daylight Saving Time (DST). For example, try London and Paris on October 26, 2024. You'll see 1 AM listed twice because at 2 AM, the clocks will "fall back" one hour due to DST.
